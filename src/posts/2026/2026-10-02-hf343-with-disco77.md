**Disco77 is back with 90 minutes of feel-good disco house, riding a steady 128-132bpm from start to finish this #FinesseFriday.**

Fresh reworks of Crystal Waters, Whitney Houston and Static Revenger sit alongside Sammy Virji & Barry Can't Swim's "I Could Be Madonna" and Ultra Nate on Crush Club's "It's Coming". There's also a double dose of Taylan, with "Keep Their Heads Ringin'" and "Fool" both off his recent [Keep Their Heads Ringin' EP on Bandcamp](https://glbdomrec.bandcamp.com/album/taylan-keep-their-heads-ringin-ep-freerep03). The set closes with Sunship remixes, the last of them in memory of Sway.
