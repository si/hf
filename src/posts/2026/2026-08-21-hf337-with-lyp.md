LYP settles into a sublime, sexy groove for HF337, blending disco-tinged house heat with deep, soulful moments throughout #FinesseFriday. Milk & Sugar crop up twice - first alongside James Hurr for the bouncy "I Like It", then solo with the sun-drenched "Let the Sun Shine" - while Riva Starr brings the warehouse energy home with a double dose of his own, teaming up with salute before going it alone. Makèz and LYMA keep the deep end flowing before Angelo Ferreri and Roller Funk close things out in style.

Big news too - House Finesse is launching its first ever radio residency this week, landing on [Colne Radio](https://colneradio.net) in Essex. Tune in and find out more over there.
