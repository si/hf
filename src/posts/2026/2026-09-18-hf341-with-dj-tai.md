**DJ Tai is back with another soulful, vocal-led session, leaning heavily into fresh remix work from the deep and disco-house underground this #FinesseFriday.**

Reel People's "It Will Be" (Ezel's Vocal Remix) opens the set in style, while Soulmekanikz put their stamp on both Brian McKnight and SWV, and Miguel Migs brings his signature deep house touch to Erykah Badu's "Bag Lady". Louie Vega and Ezel link up again on "How He Works" before Chris Nigel's "Renditions Vol 4" closes things out.
