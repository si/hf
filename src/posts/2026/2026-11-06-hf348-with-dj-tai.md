DJ Tai is back with a soulful, vocal-led session for HF348. Sir Piers and Monique Bingham open the show, Loleatta Holloway gets the Louie Vega and Yvonne Turner treatment, and Todd Terry's "Just Make That Move" keeps the middle stretch moving. Kenny Bobien turns up twice before Ralphi Rosario's "Gotta New Love" and a gospel-house closer in Kirk Franklin's "Stomp". Enjoy the ride this #FinesseFriday.
