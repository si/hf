Taylan is back with another skippy, swung house-garage session for HF344. Tim Taylor's "The Music Box" and a pair of Captain Wallop cuts keep the groove rolling, the middle stretch gets proper rugged with Catch A and Soul Mass Transit System, and Taylan drops two of his own, "Keep Their Heads Ringin'" and "Fool", before Pancratio sees us out. Enjoy the ride this #FinesseFriday.
