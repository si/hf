**This one's dedicated to Andy Driver — a friend of the residents, a man who lived for music and always made the room better just by being in it. Disco77 puts together a set full of soul, warmth and proper dancefloor joy in his memory. The kind of music Andy would have had you on your feet from the very first bar.**

From Memi P's opening welcome through gospel-tinged house, soulful classics and a Prince closer that says everything words can't, this mix was built with love. Turn it up loud and dance like he's right there with you.
