It's been a minute. Three months, in fact, since LYP last took the decks back at HF337, but he's back for HF350 with another healthy dose of uplifting disco vibes. He opens with the Schmoov! remix of Lovebirds and Stee Downes' "Want You In My Soul" before Chic get a We Are Friends re-mash on "I Want Your Love On Saturday". Riva Starr pops up twice, with "Love You Till Tomorrow" and "It Feels Much Better", Basement Jaxx drop in with "Oh My Gosh", and Ridney's "Luv Musica" in a Luca Guerrieri remix closes things out. #FinesseFriday

You can join LYP, DJ Tai, One Phat DJ and Taylan at Salt & Cellar in Chelmsford on 12th December for the first ever House Finesse Live party. It's free to attend and we'll be taking over 7pm to 1am with endless soulful, disco and gospel tunes.
