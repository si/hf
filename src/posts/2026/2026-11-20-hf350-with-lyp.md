LYP is back for HF350, opening with the Schmoov! remix of Lovebirds and Stee Downes' "Want You In My Soul" before Chic get a We Are Friends re-mash on "I Want Your Love On Saturday". Riva Starr pops up twice, with "Love You Till Tomorrow" and "It Feels Much Better", Basement Jaxx drop in with "Oh My Gosh", and Ridney's "Luv Musica" in a Luca Guerrieri remix closes things out. #FinesseFriday
