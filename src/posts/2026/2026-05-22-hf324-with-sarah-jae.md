Sarah Jae returns for her quarterly residency on House Finesse, and this time she's gone full Ibiza mode. For the bank holiday weekend, she's packed 90 minutes with floor-fillers and sun-soaked anthems from the golden era, the kind of records that made those White Isle dancefloors feel like church. Turn it up.
