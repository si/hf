**Andi King and Disco77 are back on the same decks together for the first time in nearly 15 years, going back-to-back for a full 90 minutes of disco house old and new.**

The artwork's from those original sessions all those years ago, which is why the two of them look a little different on the cover, but the music is a proper mix of fresh disco house alongside a few old favourites. Two old friends, one booth, that same chemistry from over a decade ago.

**We need your help IDing the tunes.** The lads got a bit too into the espresso martinis on the night and the tracklist didn't survive the session, so we're throwing it open to you. Reckon you know a track in the mix? Drop your IDs in the comments, and whoever comes back with the most correct answers wins a free top from the shop.
