**DJ Tai is back with a feel-good run of disco-soul reworks and vocal house favourites this #FinesseFriday.**

Dimitri From Paris puts his Super Disco Blend on Harold Melvin & The Blue Notes to open, before T-Ski Valley and a definitive extended rework of Michael Jackson keep the dancefloor moving. Louie Vega's Roots Mix of "I Love the Night" and Boris Dlugosch's "Never Enough" sit in the middle, with Kenny Dope's take on The Brand New Heavies and Bobby & Steve featuring Byron Stingily bringing it home.
