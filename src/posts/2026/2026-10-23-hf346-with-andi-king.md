**Andi King returns with a set of disco-drenched house and big vocal moments this #FinesseFriday.**

Dave 'Love' Lee opens up with "(Not) This Time Baby" before Seamus Haji and Kathy Brown invite you to "Dance with Me" and John Morales takes the floor for "All My Love". The Shapeshifters and Sharlene Hector bring the classic "Everything's Changing", Mark Picchiotti and Candice Chavez soar on "My Soul Cries Out", and Donna Allen brings the soul with "He Is the Joy". Hotmood's "Boogie Celebration" and a funky Seamus Haji reappearance keep the groove rolling, with Jonas Blue's "Girl 2.0" closing things out.
