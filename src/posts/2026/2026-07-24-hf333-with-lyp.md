**LYP is back with another sublime, sexy dose of house music for HF333 — and this one carries extra weight.** The Driver Bros., LYP collab "On The Floor 2026" is a heartfelt dedication to the late Andy Driver, who we lost recently — another emotional moment for the House Finesse family, and a track we're proud to give its moment here.

Beyond that tribute, this is prime LYP territory: Basement Jaxx's "Oh My Gosh" rubs shoulders with fresh cuts from Bella Ora and Jazzwaves, Ultra Nate and Riva Starr keep things deep and irresistible, and Nalin & Kane's "Beachball" gets the Someguy treatment to close things out in style.

Sublime, sexy, and full of heart. Rest in peace, Andy.
