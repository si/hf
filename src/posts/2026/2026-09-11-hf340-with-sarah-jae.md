Sarah Jae is back after a summer away, and she's made it count. HF340 is a deep tech and old-school electro affair, new cuts sat right alongside nostalgia, opening with Cam Stockman's "Get Close" and running through The BeatBoy's cheeky "Sex & Drugs" and Oscar G & Ralph Falcon's "Dark Beat".

The back end brings the heat, Biscits' "Wrong Is Right" and Vintage Culture & Roddy Lima's "Analog Ascent" keep the tech-house energy up, before Âme's timeless "Rej" closes things out in style. Good to have her back this #FinesseFriday.
