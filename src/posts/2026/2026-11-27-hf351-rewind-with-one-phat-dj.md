**This #FinesseFriday we're digging into the archives for a rewind to One Phat DJ's guest mix for Rachael Depp's eclectic 1 Indie Nation podcast, first shared back in April 2022.**

It opens with Duane Harden and the House Gospel Choir on "You Don't Know Me" and the Michael Gray remix of Blaze and Barbara Tucker's "Most Precious Love". Kathy Brown turns up on "Don't Turn Off The Lights", Daniel Steinberg's "2001" is in there too, and the Disco Sparks mix of Lee Wilson, Bubs and Clavette's "You Better Live It" closes things out. #FinesseFriday

You can join LYP, DJ Tai, One Phat DJ and Taylan at Salt & Cellar in Chelmsford on 12th December for the first ever House Finesse Live party. It's free to attend and we'll be taking over 7pm to 1am with endless soulful, disco and gospel tunes.
