**DJ Tai returns to House Finesse with a soulful, vocal-led session that mixes disco-tinged remix work with plenty of dancefloor heat this #FinesseFriday.**

Tony Momrelle's "My Paradise" gets a rework from Louie Vega, who returns later in the set to flip Jasper Street Co.'s "Praying For You" too. Dave Lee (ZR) stretches out Donae'o, Omar and Lemar's "Nights Like This", while Anna-Marie Johnson & Mark Francis and Merlin Bobb bring the vocal heat elsewhere in the mix.

DJ Tai closes things out in style with a Conan Liquid re-edit of Janet Jackson's "When I Think Of You" - a fitting finish to a set full of quality.
