# 15 Fatboy Slim Tracks You Had NO IDEA Were Him (Disco77's Deep Cuts)

Fatboy Slim's playing the park this Saturday, so we cornered our resident Norman Cook obsessive, Disco77, and he did NOT hold back — 15 tracks deep into a WhatsApp rabbit hole he twice apologised for ("I went full ADHD, didn't I"). From bootlegs he made under other people's names to a remix that may or may not have quietly ended up sampled in the Futurama theme tune, here's the full list, in his own words.

🎧 [Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/15tt1kzgBXArsat1H9Hkrg) · [Listen on YouTube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLYCT3BMx5ZuA&si=TLNHUiY1wjwx7gvY)

1. **Beats International – Dub Be Good to Me (1990)**
   *"Some mental tunes. Here's my first two Norman Cook non-Housemartin songs."*
   https://youtu.be/LCzgsZi_zfg

2. **Eric B. & Rakim – Paid in Full (7 Minutes of Madness Remix)**
   *"People forget Norman Cook did THIS remix — which predates Beats International."*
   https://youtu.be/z5iiEtFL69I

3. **Pizzaman – Trippin on Sunshine**
   *"This was one of my entry points to him as well."*
   https://youtu.be/OHwrAAxomew

4. **Everybody Needs a 303**
   *"Bought this the day I went to see La Haine (my fave film of all time at the cinema) — the first Fatboy Slim tune."* (Si reckons that's 1995.)
   https://youtu.be/fYAR5VdqvHk

5. **F.C. Kahuna – What Is Kahuna? (Fatboy Slim's "Smell the Kahuna" Mix)**
   *"My fave remix — transports me right back to the mid-90s."*
   https://youtu.be/9k10nRMpddM

6. **Fatboy Slim – Song for Lindy**
   *"This was the Congress '40 Miles' bootleg he made for Lindy Layton, singer of Beats International."*
   https://youtu.be/3VpoyWKM9TY

7. **Mighty Dub Katz – Just Another Groove (2008 Remaster)**
   *"This is one of the best things he ever made."*
   https://youtu.be/q4_skUkoLTc

8. **Pierre Henry – Psyché Rock (Fatboy Slim Remix)**
   *"The Futurama theme tune was ripped off one of his remixes." — this is the one.*
   https://youtu.be/RilGl-IF7zw

9. **I'm a Disco Dancer (Fatboy Slim Remix)**
   *"Last one for now...." (narrator: it was not the last one)*
   https://youtu.be/Kx80TlXvzX4

10. **Deeds Plus Thoughts – The World's Made Up of This and That (Fatboy Slim Mix)**
    *"Here's a really obscure one. I think he must have used the same session notes for this Wildchild remix. Always played this one into Renegade Master."*
    https://youtu.be/mbUdtmC-ydM

11. **Markus Nikolai – Bushes (Norman Cook Remix)**
    *"The remix was brilliant. Ended up picking this up from Blackmarket as it was impossible to get on vinyl."*
    https://youtu.be/42gBMtL9zhE

12. **A Tribe Called Quest – I Left My Wallet in El Segundo (Norman Cook Vampire Mix)**
    *"Forgot this one. I bought this aged 13."*
    https://youtu.be/emRnrIJyzbk

13. **Peplab – Ride the Pony (Norman Cook Club Mix)**
    *"Distinctive! What a great label in the late 90s."*
    https://youtu.be/Zxnxj-SeYC8

14. **Mighty Dub Katz – Let the Drums Speak**
    *"Didn't want to go super obvious, but I still love this so much."*
    https://youtu.be/CtwdA2WcS5E

15. **Bootsy Collins – Party Lick-a-Ble's (Norman Cook Version)**
    *"Used to play this quite a lot. Usually mixing it with Armand Funk Phenomenon."*
    https://youtu.be/Fn524e67yqs

All non-obvious, all genuinely him — 15 deep. See how many you can spot before Saturday.
