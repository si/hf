## 🚶 Si's 50km walk for Dementia UK

Si (aka One Phat DJ) is walking 50km around the Kent Downs next weekend, Saturday 10th October, to raise money for Dementia UK.

He's doing it for his dad, whose dementia has got progressively worse over the past year. It's now hard for him to see people, even his own family. If you can spare anything, it'd mean a lot, and every pound helps.

→ [Sponsor Si on JustGiving](https://www.justgiving.com/page/si-kent-50)

## 🎸 What we've been watching: Nile Rodgers

The whole crew has been working through a documentary on Nile Rodgers, and it's a cracker. If you want to know where half the records you love really came from, start here.

→ [Nile Rodgers: From Adversity to Icon | Full Episode | Once In A Lifetime Sessions (YouTube)](https://youtu.be/Nu6A2ZI-SXo)

What blew our minds? Luther Vandross singing backing vocals on "Everybody Dance". Absolutely unreal to hear it stripped back.
