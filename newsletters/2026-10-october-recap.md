# Five Fridays, five mixes, and Sarah Jae heads to Fabric

## Intro

October flew by, and it was a busy one: five Fridays, five brilliant mixes. We've got a Disco77 set with a tribute at the end, a skippy house-garage ride from Taylan, DJ Tai going full disco-soul, Andi King bringing the vocals, and Sarah Jae closing the month with a set made entirely of 2026 heaters. Here's everything you might have missed.

## What's been playing?

### HF343 with Disco77

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf343"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF343_with_Disco77.webp" alt="HF343 with Disco77 cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*90 minutes of feel-good disco house, locked at 128-132bpm.*

Disco77 kicks off the month with fresh reworks of Crystal Waters, Whitney Houston and Static Revenger, plus Sammy Virji & Barry Can't Swim's "I Could Be Madonna" and Ultra Nate on Crush Club's "It's Coming". There's a double dose of Taylan too, and the set closes with a pair of Sunship remixes, the last of them in memory of Sway.

→ [Listen to Disco77 now](https://housefinesse.com/hf343)

### HF344 with Taylan

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf344"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF344_with_Taylan.webp" alt="HF344 with Taylan cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*Skippy, swung house-garage that gets funkier and weightier as it goes.*

Tim Taylor's "The Music Box" and a pair of Captain Wallop cuts keep the groove rolling, then the middle stretch gets properly rugged with Catch A and Soul Mass Transit System. Taylan drops two of his own, "Keep Their Heads Ringin'" and "Fool", before Pancratio sees us out.

→ [Listen to Taylan now](https://housefinesse.com/hf344)

### HF345 with DJ Tai

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf345"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF345_with_DJ_Tai.webp" alt="HF345 with DJ Tai cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*Disco-soul reworks and vocal house favourites, all feel-good.*

Dimitri From Paris puts his Super Disco Blend on Harold Melvin & The Blue Notes to open, and a definitive extended rework of Michael Jackson's "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough" keeps things moving. Louie Vega's Roots Mix of "I Love the Night" and Boris Dlugosch's "Never Enough" sit in the middle, before Kenny Dope's take on The Brand New Heavies and Bobby & Steve with Byron Stingily bring it home.

→ [Listen to DJ Tai now](https://housefinesse.com/hf345)

### HF346 with Andi King

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf346"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF346_with_Andi_King.webp" alt="HF346 with Andi King cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*Disco-drenched house and big vocal moments.*

Dave 'Love' Lee opens with "(Not) This Time Baby", then Seamus Haji and Kathy Brown invite you to "Dance with Me". The Shapeshifters and Sharlene Hector bring the classic "Everything's Changing", Mark Picchiotti and Candice Chavez soar on "My Soul Cries Out", and Jonas Blue's "Girl 2.0" closes it out.

→ [Listen to Andi King now](https://housefinesse.com/hf346)

### HF347 with Sarah Jae

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf347"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF347_with_Sarah_Jae.webp" alt="HF347 with Sarah Jae cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*Sweaty, bass-heavy 2026 heaters only.*

Every track in this one was released this year. Crazy P's "Pawa" vocal dub sets the tone, Rocco Universal's "Space Chakra" and "Mutiny on the Sex Raft" get weird in the best way, and Purple Disco Machine's "Disco Cherry" lands right on time.

And if you want to catch Sarah Jae live, she's playing the iconic Fabric in London on 21 November for the Fump 15th anniversary.

→ [Listen to Sarah Jae now](https://housefinesse.com/hf347)
→ [Get Fabric tickets](https://www.fabriclondon.com/event/fabric-parallelle-live-krystal-klear-inland-knights-fifi-forte-tommy-gold-goosey-more)
