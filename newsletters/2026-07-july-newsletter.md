### Subject / Post title
**5 shows, 1 tribute, and a chance to win a House Finesse cap — your July round-up**

### Body

Five Fridays, five shows — July was a big one for House Finesse. Here's everything you might have missed, plus a competition and a summer offer for the VIP Club.

---

**HF330 — One Phat DJ**
![HF330 with One Phat DJ](https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF330_with_OPDJ.jpg)

*Disco heat, soulful grooves, and not a filler track in sight.*

Your OG is back on the decks for HF330 with a set that's all soulful house and disco heat, front to back. George Smeddles brings the House Gospel Choir along for "Angels", DJ Spen teams up with Tasha LaRae and Gary Hudgins for a proper deep cut, and Spiller & Sophie Ellis-Bextor's "Groovejet" gets reworked for a new generation.

👉 [Full track listing & details](https://housefinesse.com/hf330)

---

**HF331 — Andi King (Live from Glitterball)**
![HF331 with Andi King](https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF331_with_Andi_King.jpeg)

*90 minutes, live, back-to-back with his Espresso Brother.*

Heading back to end of May for a full 90 minutes live from Glitterball, Andi King is joined by his Espresso Brother, Alex, for a proper back-to-back. Two selectors, one booth, and a dancefloor that didn't stop moving. This one's also a tribute to our late friend Andy Driver, who sadly passed away recently — this set is dedicated to him and everything he brought to the House Finesse family.

👉 [Full details](https://housefinesse.com/hf331)

---

**HF332 — DJ Tai**
![HF332 with DJ Tai](https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF332_with_Taiwo.jpg)

*"Dirty Disco Funk" — thirteen tracks of pure floor-filling groove.*

DJ Tai is back with his Dirty Disco Funk mix, heavy on the edits and remixes from the disco underground's finest re-touchers — Dimitri From Paris, Los Charly's Orchestra, Grant Nelson's rework of Jamiroquai's "Runaway", and a closing run through Louie Vega, DJ Spen and Muthafunkaz. Dirty by name, funky by nature.

👉 [Full track listing & details](https://housefinesse.com/hf332)

---

**HF333 — LYP**
![HF333 with LYP](https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF333_with_LYP.jpeg)

*Sublime, sexy house — and one with extra weight.*

LYP is back with another sublime, sexy dose of house music, and this one carries extra weight: the Driver Bros. x LYP collab "On The Floor 2026" is a heartfelt dedication to the late Andy Driver. Beyond that tribute, it's prime LYP territory — Basement Jaxx's "Oh My Gosh", fresh cuts from Bella Ora and Jazzwaves, and Nalin & Kane's "Beachball" getting the Someguy treatment to close.

👉 [Full track listing & details](https://housefinesse.com/hf333)

---

**HF334 — Taylan**
![HF334 with Taylan](https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF334_with_Taylan.jpg)

*Deep and atmospheric, building into high energy — this #FinesseFriday.*

Taylan's back on House Finesse after a little time away, having spent it soaking up inspiration from raves and gigs of his own. That comes through in HF334 — a mix that eases in deep and atmospheric before building into high energy territory, running through cuts from Noah Skelton, Pierre Marty and Arthur Yard on the way to a Karizma & DJ Spen closer.

👉 [Full track listing & details](https://housefinesse.com/hf334)
