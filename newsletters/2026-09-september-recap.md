# A 15-year reunion, and five shows to catch up on

## Intro

September's been a busy one. Five shows landed, including a back-to-back that's been nearly 15 years in the making, plus Sarah Jae and One Phat DJ both back from their summer breaks. Here's everything you might have missed.

## What's been playing?

### HF339 with Andi King & Disco77

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf339"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF339_with_Andi_King_Disco77.jpg" alt="HF339 with Andi King and Disco77 cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*Two old friends, one booth, 90 minutes of disco house.*

Andi King and Disco77 are on the same decks together for the first time in nearly 15 years. The artwork's from the original sessions, hence the slightly younger faces. There's one catch: the espresso martinis won, and the tracklist didn't survive the night. Think you know a tune? Drop your IDs in the comments and the person with the most correct answers wins a free top from the shop.

→ [Listen to Andi King & Disco77 now](https://housefinesse.com/hf339)

### HF340 with Sarah Jae

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf340"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF340_with_Sarah_Jae.jpg" alt="HF340 with Sarah Jae cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*Back after a summer away, and she's made it count.*

Deep tech meets old-school electro, with new cuts sat right next to the nostalgia. Opens with Cam Stockman's "Get Close", takes in Oscar G & Ralph Falcon's "Dark Beat" and Vintage Culture & Roddy Lima's "Analog Ascent", then closes with Âme's timeless "Rej".

→ [Listen to Sarah Jae now](https://housefinesse.com/hf340)

### HF341 with DJ Tai

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf341"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF341_with_DJ_Tai.webp" alt="HF341 with DJ Tai cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*Soulful, remix-heavy and full of vocal heat.*

Reel People's "It Will Be" (Ezel's Vocal Remix) opens things up, Soulmekanikz rework both Brian McKnight and SWV, and Miguel Migs gives Erykah Badu's "Bag Lady" the deep house treatment. Louie Vega and Ezel link up again on "How He Works" before Chris Nigel's "Renditions Vol 4" sees it out.

→ [Listen to DJ Tai now](https://housefinesse.com/hf341)

### HF342 with One Phat DJ

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf342"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF342_with_OPDJ.webp" alt="HF342 with One Phat DJ cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*New meets classic, soulful house front to back.*

Fresh cuts from Georges & Fleur De Mur and The Sunburst Band open the set, then it tips into a run of reworked classics: Kathy Brown, Soul Central's "Strings of Life" and CeCe Peniston's "Finally" among them.

→ [Listen to One Phat DJ now](https://housefinesse.com/hf342)

### HF343 with Disco77

<a href="https://housefinesse.com/hf343"><img src="https://housefinesse.com/img/cover-images/HF343_with_Disco77.webp" alt="HF343 with Disco77 cover art" style="max-width:100%;display:block;margin-bottom:12px;"/></a>

*90 minutes of feel-good disco house, 128-132bpm all the way.*

Fresh reworks of Crystal Waters, Whitney Houston and Static Revenger sit alongside Sammy Virji & Barry Can't Swim's "I Could Be Madonna" and Ultra Nate on Crush Club's "It's Coming". There's a double dose of Taylan too, with two cuts from his [Keep Their Heads Ringin' EP](https://glbdomrec.bandcamp.com/album/taylan-keep-their-heads-ringin-ep-freerep03) on Bandcamp. The set closes with Sunship remixes, the last in memory of Sway.

→ [Listen to Disco77 now](https://housefinesse.com/hf343)

## Si's 50km walk for dementia

Si (aka One Phat DJ) is walking 50km around the Kent Downs next weekend, Saturday 10th October, to raise money for [Dementia UK](https://www.justgiving.com/page/si-kent-50).

He's doing it for his dad, whose dementia has got progressively worse over the past year. It's now hard for him to see people, even his own family. If you can spare anything, it'd mean a lot, and every pound helps.

→ [Sponsor Si on JustGiving](https://www.justgiving.com/page/si-kent-50)
